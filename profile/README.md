### 📑 About Us

Malwarecraft is an educational organization dedicated to researching and developing malware, security tools, and game cheats for educational and testing purposes. 

Our goal is to explore low-level programming, reverse engineering, software protection mechanisms, and offensive security concepts in controlled environments.

---

### 🛠️ Areas of Focus

- **🦠 Malware Research & PoCs:** Low-level payloads, evasion techniques, and proof-of-concept software.
- **🛡️ Security Tools:** Analysis frameworks, reverse engineering utilities, and testing tools.
- **🎮 Game Modifications & Cheats:** Memory manipulation, hook injection, and anti-cheat analysis.

---

### 💻 Tech Stack & Focus Areas

`C` • `C++` • `Assembly (x86/x64)` • `Python` • `Reverse Engineering` • `Win32 API` • `Kernel Drivers`
